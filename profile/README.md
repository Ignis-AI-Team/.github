# Ignis

Product systems builder for AI products and operational pipelines.

I turn a messy business workflow into a focused, production-ready system that your team owns.

## What that means

**Blueprint.** An unclear problem becomes a workflow, an architecture, an MVP boundary, a risk register and a fixed build plan. Paid discovery, before a complex build.

**MVP build.** One primary user and one valuable workflow, with a real interface, a data model and a production release.

**Operational pipelines.** Manual hand-offs are replaced with explicit state, human approval, monitoring, recovery and a readable run history.

## How the work is run

Human approval and auditable state come before any consequential automated action.

A claim carries its source, its date and its sample size, or it does not ship. An agent reporting success is a claim, not a verification.

Every credential is rotated at handover. The repository, the hosting and the database end up in your accounts.

## This organisation

Client repositories are private and are transferred to the client at handover. Public repositories here are forks of third-party tooling the practice uses, not work built for clients.

There are no client case studies yet, and nothing here pretends otherwise.
